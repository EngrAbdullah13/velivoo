1. How we plan to use Amazon SES

Velivoo is a permission-based email marketing platform. Business customers verify their own sending domain in SES through our product. We send marketing and lifecycle email only to opted-in contacts, From the customer’s verified domain (e.g. user@customer.com).

We do not send unsolicited bulk email, purchased lists, or scraped addresses.

2. Verified identity

We use verified domain identities (not single addresses). Platform domain velivoo.com and each customer domain are verified in SES with DKIM and custom MAIL FROM before sending is enabled.

3. How often we send

  • Starting volume: approximately 50–200 emails per day
  • Expected growth (3–6 months): up to 1,000–5,000 emails per day
  • Sends are triggered by customer automations and campaigns — not continuous bulk blasts

4. How we maintain recipient lists

  • Contacts are added only with documented opt-in (form consent or import with consent source/date/method)
  • Consent and subscription status are stored per contact in our database
  • Contacts without granted marketing consent are blocked before send — the message is skipped and never submitted to SES
  • Hard bounces, spam complaints, and unsubscribes add the contact to a protected suppression list immediately
  • We do not use purchased or rented lists

5. Bounce handling

When SES reports a bounce, our backend processes it automatically:

  • Hard (permanent) bounce: contact is suppressed; all pending emails to that address are cancelled; future marketing sends are blocked before SES submission
  • Soft (transient) bounce: event is logged; no permanent suppression
Bounce handling flow (backend)

6. Complaint handling

When a recipient marks email as spam, SES sends a Complaint event. Our backend then:

  1. Verifies the SNS notification and saves it durably
  2. Matches the event to the original sent message
  3. Records a complaint delivery event and sets message status to complained
  4. Creates a protected global suppression for that contact (cannot be manually removed)
  5. Cancels any other pending marketing messages to that contact
  6. Blocks all future marketing sends to that contact at pre-send policy check

Complaint status takes priority over delivery — a later “delivered” event cannot overwrite a complaint.

Complaint handling flow (backend)

7. Unsubscribe handling

Every marketing email includes a visible Unsubscribe link with a signed token unique to that recipient.

  • Click link (GET): shows a confirmation page — viewing the link alone does not unsubscribe (protects against email scanners)
  • Confirm (POST): consent is withdrawn, global unsubscribe suppression is created, pending messages are cancelled
  • Future sends: blocked automatically before SES submission
  • Duplicate unsubscribe clicks are handled safely (idempotent)
Unsubscribe flow (backend)

8. Pre-send check (before email reaches SES)

Before any marketing message is submitted to SES, our system checks:

  • Marketing consent granted
  • No active suppression (bounce, complaint, or unsubscribe)
  • Valid recipient email
  • Sender domain verified and ready

If any check fails, the message is skipped — it is not sent to SES.

We align with the AWS Acceptable Use Policy, AWS Service Terms, and Amazon SES best practices. Bounces, complaints, and unsubscribes are handled automatically by our backend — not manually.
